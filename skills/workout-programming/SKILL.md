# Workout programming

- **Split by frequency**: 2-3 days/week -> full body; 4 -> upper/lower; 5-6 -> push/pull/legs.
- **Volume**: ~10-20 hard sets per muscle per week; beginners at the low end.
- **Intensity**: strength 3-6 reps @ RPE 7-9; hypertrophy 6-15 reps @ RPE 7-9; 1-3 reps in reserve.
- **Progression (double progression)**: work in a rep range (e.g. 8-12). When all sets hit the
  top of the range at the target RPE, add the smallest load increment (2.5 kg upper / 5 kg lower)
  and restart at the bottom of the range.
- **Deload** every 4-8 weeks or after 2 stalled sessions: cut sets ~40%, keep load.
- **What to track** per set: date, exercise, weight, reps, RPE. Derived: estimated 1RM
  (Epley: w * (1 + reps/30)), weekly volume (sets x reps x weight) per muscle group,
  personal records, bodyweight trend (7-day average).
- Warm-up: 5 min easy cardio + 2-3 ramp-up sets for the first compound lift.
